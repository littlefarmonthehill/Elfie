Driftless Business Solutions ("DBS")
DBS helps business owners focus on the work they love by taking care of website, messaging, and digital management — so the Client can spend more time with customers and less time on admin tasks.

Scope of Work

DBS will provide services to support Minnesota Building Contractors in:

  • Increasing residential service inquiries and referrals
  • Clearly expressing the Client's vision, mission, and messaging
  • Showcasing completed work through before-and-after galleries, photos, and videos
  • Providing lead capture and contact forms
  • Creating content assets ready for marketing and social media

Work can be front-loaded in the Initial Development package or spread out over time with Ongoing Support, depending on the Client's chosen package and priorities.

1.1   Initial Development

DBS will build a custom public-facing website focused on residential services, using the Client's existing pages and content as the foundation, and including:

  • Custom design and layout
  • Mobile-friendly display
  • Copywriting aligned with vision, mission, and residential messaging
  • Lead capture and contact forms
  • Project showcase (galleries, photos, videos) — scope varies by package
  • Organized content and messaging assets ready for marketing or social media
