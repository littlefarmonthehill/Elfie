Repricing Score Metrics

1. Price Ceiling Ratio

What it measures
How much historical price upside exists compared to your current listing price. This helps identify items that may be underpriced relative to past market sales.

Calculation

price_ceiling_ratio = market_highest_sold_price / our_listed_price

Interpretation
	•	> 1.0 → potential room to increase price
	•	≈ 1.0 → price near historical market highs
	•	< 1.0 → your price may already exceed typical market highs

⸻

2. Demand Velocity

What it measures
The rate at which items sell relative to available supply, indicating market demand strength.

Calculation

demand_velocity = market_quantity_sold / market_quantity_supply

Interpretation
	•	Higher score → stronger demand relative to supply
	•	Lower score → slower market turnover

⸻

3. Market Scarcity Index

What it measures
How scarce the item currently is in the market based on available supply.

Calculation

market_scarcity_index = 1 / market_quantity_supply

Interpretation
	•	Higher score → low supply (scarce item)
	•	Lower score → large supply (common item)

⸻

4. Undercut Ratio

What it measures
Your price position compared to the lowest current market listing, helping identify when you are being undercut by competitors.

Calculation

undercut_ratio = our_listed_price / market_lowest_price

Interpretation
	•	> 1.0 → you are priced above the lowest listing (being undercut)
	•	≈ 1.0 → you are priced at market floor
	•	< 1.0 → you are cheaper than competitors

⸻

Combined Repricing Score

Repricing Opportunity Score

What it measures
An overall pricing opportunity score combining price upside, demand strength, scarcity, and competitive positioning.

Calculation

repricing_opportunity_score =
    (price_ceiling_ratio * 0.4) +
    (demand_velocity * 0.3) +
    (market_scarcity_index * 0.2) +
    ((1 / undercut_ratio) * 0.1)

Purpose

Items with the highest score represent the best candidates for repricing, balancing:
	•	potential price increases
	•	strong demand
	•	limited supply
	•	competitive market position
